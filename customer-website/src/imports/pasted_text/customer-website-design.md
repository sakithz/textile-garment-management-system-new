Design a premium, highly creative, modern and visually impressive PUBLIC CUSTOMER WEBSITE for a Sri Lankan Textile & Garment Manufacturing Company.

IMPORTANT:
This is NOT the internal ERP/admin dashboard.
This is the customer-facing public website and customer portal.

The internal company management system is a separate application with role-based dashboards.
This website should focus on:
- Brand identity
- Garment products
- Services
- Custom garment manufacturing
- Promotions and offers
- Customer engagement
- Online order requests
- Order tracking
- Customer account management

The website should feel like a premium modern fashion-tech / textile manufacturing brand, not a generic corporate website.

==================================================
1. DESIGN DIRECTION
==================================================

Create a visually stunning, premium, modern textile and garment company website.

Design inspiration:
- Premium fashion brands
- Modern manufacturing companies
- Contemporary SaaS interactions
- Editorial fashion websites
- High-end product showcases

The design should combine:
- Fashion
- Textile
- Technology
- Manufacturing
- Sustainability
- Professionalism

Use large typography, beautiful garment/textile imagery, subtle animations, elegant transitions, strong visual hierarchy and immersive sections.

The design should be attractive enough to immediately communicate:
"Premium garment manufacturing with modern technology."

Avoid:
- Generic Bootstrap-looking layouts
- Basic corporate templates
- Excessive cards
- Overcrowded sections
- Excessive gradients
- Cheap-looking animations
- Stock-template appearance

==================================================

==================================================

Create a sophisticated textile-inspired visual identity.

Use:
- Warm neutral backgrounds

- Elegant gradients only where useful

Use high-quality fashion and textile imagery.

Typography:
- Large editorial headings
- Clean modern body typography
- Strong contrast between headings and supporting text

Use generous whitespace.

Create a consistent design system.
